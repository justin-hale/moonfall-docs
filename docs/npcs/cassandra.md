# Cassandra

*Factory Floor Worker • Eldoran Capital • Elspeth's Friend*

## Overview

**Cassandra** works on the floor of the Eldoran [spaceport factory](/locations/spaceport-factory). [Elspeth](/player-characters/elspeth), working there undercover, made friends with her in Session 60. Cassandra loves tea. The day after Elspeth met her, Cassandra held a birthday party at a lake in the park, and the party used it as cover for Silas to steal a research scientist's lab coat and passcode. Her whole shift was due to be laid off on the morning the party struck the factory (Session 63). What became of her is not recorded.

## Session History

- **[Session 60](/sessions/session-60)** — On the factory floor, where Elspeth works in disguise and the workers call her "Tracy", the crew sing happy birthday to Cassandra and invite Elspeth to her lakeside party the next day. Meanwhile the party overhears an administrator planning to fire the whole floor crew during the factory's retooling shutdown.
- **[Session 62](/sessions/session-62)** — At her lakeside birthday party, Elspeth, calling herself "Stephanie", gives her a tea set ("This tea set is me... Did I tell you I love tea?"). Her friend Gracie confirms the layoffs. While the guests swim, Silas steals the lab coat of a research scientist who came as a guest's plus-one.
