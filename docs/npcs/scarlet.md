# Scarlet

*Elf • Engineer and Spellcaster • Keeper of the Pocket Village*

## Overview

**Scarlet** is an elf engineer and spellcaster who travels with the party. She is Topher's own character: he plays her as a player character when someone else guest-DMs (Interlude 12, Session 44) and voices her as the DM the rest of the time. She did much of the dimensional work on the [pocket village](/locations/pocket-village), carries it folded into a capsule, and as of Session 63 holds the shrunken sun stolen from the Eldoran [spaceport factory](/locations/spaceport-factory) in her *Leomund's Secret Chest*. The party watches her closely, because in another timeline a time gun destroyed a whole universe, and they fear she might build one.

She is not [Red](/player-characters/red) and not [Elspeth](/player-characters/elspeth).

## Background

- **Origins:** In Session 62 she told Elspeth she is three hundred years old and was born under an apple tree. She keeps her birthday somewhere in late summer or early autumn.
- **Her alternate self:** In Interlude 11 the party met a different Scarlet in the wrecked world of the Quizzites. That Scarlet was a short blonde elf and former assassin who held her floating world together with enchanted paint. She, the chronomancer Marcus and the world tree Matthew Broadtwig had spent two millennia trying to undo a time gun disaster. She gave the party a magical paint set that alters reality when used on maps or canvases.
- **This world's Scarlet:** In Session 25 the party met the Scarlet of their own dimension. Her role is reversed: here she is the scientist researching time and space, and Marcus is the painter. The party talked her out of her temporal experiments and into helping with their pocket-dimension workspace.
- **Engineer:** "I'm an engineer. I have a PhD. Even worse" (Session 44). Her spells in the field have included Fireball, Slow and Haste (Session 44) and *Leomund's Secret Chest* (Session 63).

## The Pocket Village

Scarlet researched the dimensional side of the project that became the pocket village:

- She and Silas made interdimensional glass spheres for it in the party's van, the [Zoomfall](/items/zoomfall) (Session 35). She worked on it with Bru and Elspeth after Arc 2, when it was about half finished (Session 37; the recaps of Sessions 37–38 call the project "the Stellaro").
- She asked for the reality stones from Red's old Goliath village to stabilise its interior (Session 38).
- At Iro's workshop in Greyport she ran the first trials in the catalytic chamber (Session 44). After it began forming hills and streams on its own, she calibrated it with [Victor](/npcs/victor)'s instruments and opened it (Session 45).
- When void beings attacked inside, she held the space together from outside (Session 46). She then locked the door with a key and folded the village into a small capsule that she carries (Session 47).

## The Time Gun

The [time gun](/items/time-gun) that destroyed the Quizzites' universe gets built in every timeline by someone: Marcus in one, an alternate Scarlet in another. Because this world's Scarlet is the time-and-space scientist, the party worries she might build one too. Silas raised this in Session 38, and in Session 63 the party agreed to keep steering her away from it.

## Relationships

- **Silas Fairbanks:** Warned her not to be inspired by interdimensional travel (Interlude 12) and keeps her off time guns. In Session 60 he told her she is part of the team and steps up when needed.
- **Bru:** Works with her on engineering projects and gave her brown for her birthday. After she stored the sun he said, "I could kiss you right now, Scarlet, but I won't cuz you're disgusting."
- **Elspeth Cooper:** Confided in her about following [Lady Viper](/npcs/lady-viper). Scarlet promised to keep it secret and told Silas at once (Session 62).
- **[Leliana](/player-characters/leliana):** Gave her a hand-braided string necklace, the only jewellery Scarlet owns (Session 53).

- **[Session 62](/sessions/session-62)** — She goes with Elspeth to the Tony meeting and passes Elspeth's secret straight to Silas. She tells Elspeth she is three hundred years old and was born under an apple tree, and helps Elspeth into a swimsuit disguise at the lake party.
