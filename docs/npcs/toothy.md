# Toothy

*Twilight Company Safehouse Keeper • Eldoran Capital*

## Overview

**Toothy** runs a [Twilight Company](/organizations/twilight-company) safehouse in the [Eldoran](/locations/eldoran) capital. It is room 238 on the third floor of an apartment building on the western hill, next to an old church that is now a community center, with a view over the [barracks](/locations/eldoran-barracks), the [spaceport](/locations/spaceport-factory), and the Guilded Esplanade. [George](/npcs/george) sent the party there in [Session 58](/sessions/session-58), and it has been their base in the capital ever since. As of Session 63 he is still keeping the safehouse.

## The Safehouse

The apartment has six bedrooms around a common room, with a big window looking out to the sea, plus a shared kitchen and bathroom. The party got two bunk rooms, and the other bedrooms were already full of other residents. Toothy keeps snacks in the fridge and holds a family breakfast and dinner every day. Wake-up is at 7:00 and breakfast at 7:30, and he asks guests to contribute. On the morning of the Session 62 operations, he had a big pot of coffee brewed and plain buttery croissants ready.

Toothy is also the party's line to George. George cannot use a sending stone without being traced, so messages go through Toothy, sent a day ahead. George said he had been working mostly through Toothy and [Finnegan](/npcs/finnegan).

## Character

In the Session 58 transcript, the DM describes him as having big teeth. He is brusque and makes no claim to understand the operation: he says he "just runs this place," and of the bomb instructions, "I don't understand this" stuff. [Silas](/player-characters/silas) judged him on his name alone. Toothy has one firm house rule: [Victor](/npcs/victor) does not stay there. *"He stayed here once and he ruined his room."*

## Role in the Strike

Victor had sent a supply list and bomb instructions ahead through Toothy. In Session 58, Toothy pulled the page out of his pocket: a sheet torn from a religious text, with bomb instructions in the margins. He also asked whether George had mentioned the nitrogen on his supply list. The party's room was stocked with pressure-cooker pots, fuse wire, fireworks, dynamite, and gunpowder. Planning for the three-target strike ran out of his apartment. The [Session 59](/sessions/session-59) morning briefing with the private detective Ben Boulage was held there, and in [Session 62](/sessions/session-62) Silas and Finnegan planned the operation there.
