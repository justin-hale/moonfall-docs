# Crowley

*Fae • Dealmaker • Collector of Heroes*

## Overview

**Mr. Crowley** is a fae who deals in bargains and has been the party's recurring otherworldly enemy since Session 9. He wears a white suit and an opera mask and has pointed teeth, and he is charming in a disturbing way. The party gave him a fox totem that let him attack the fae spirit Toonlock, then helped Toonlock escape him, and since then Crowley has treated them as personal enemies. He knows their names, including Red's true name. He last claimed to be holding Caspian, the Winter King's assistant (Interlude 6). In Session 51 he lured the retired wrestling champion [Gary](/npcs/gary) through a portal, and neither has been seen since.

## Known Deeds

- **Bori's bargain (Session 9):** Crowley took the consciousness of a boy, Bori, who traded it to save his mother's life. He offered to restore Bori in exchange for a fox-shaped fae totem from a shrine in the woods. The party delivered it and Helisanna shook on the deal. Elspeth identified the totem as a fae anchor for the spirit Toonlock.
- **Toonlock's realm (Interludes 1–6):** Crowley used the totem to reach Toonlock's domain and began tearing it apart. The party gathered a mask, a priest's stole and a dagger so Toonlock could cut his realm free of the astral tree. At the last moment Crowley froze time and offered the party one last chance to back out. When they refused, he told them he had captured Caspian and knew all their names. Toonlock finished the ritual anyway.
- **Caspian (Session 18):** As Caspian, the Winter King's assistant, left Milstrom after the solstice, Crowley caught her in a starless void, greeted her as an "old friend" and blew out his candle.
- **The cooking show (Interludes 8–9):** He hosted and judged a dream cooking competition in which the party were the ingredients, and lost control of it when they derailed the final challenge.
- **Velz'eth (Interlude 10):** The chaos god Velz'eth told the party that Crowley, under the name "Fergus the Serpent Tongue", cheated him out of his power and left him trapped. Velz'eth said Crowley is working with the "Moon Queen", Eldora, to build a world of "cold steel and blind salvation". Velz'eth enchanted the party's items with "seeds of rupture" meant for use against Crowley and Eldora.
- **Gary (Session 51):** Crowley waited until Gary retired, since a reigning champion could only have been challenged in the ring, then called to him from a portal in a Greyport alley: "Say, Gary, would you like a piece of candy?" Gary stepped through.
