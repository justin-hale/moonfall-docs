# Gold Road

*Trade Route • High Forge to Eldoran*

## Overview

The **Gold Road** is the main road north out of [High Forge](/locations/high-forge), eventually reaching the Eldoran capital, [Eldoran](/locations/eldoran). Before it gets there it branches off toward [Greyport](/locations/greyport). The split comes just before a mountain pass, past the foothill village below the Goliath village on [Grimvar's Spine](/locations/grimvars-spine); a crossroads town grew up at that bottleneck because everyone leaving the region has to pass through it (the DM's description in Session 38).

The road has appeared only in passing: as the route of a caravan job in Session 1, as Zeke's escape route in Session 13, and as the party's road out of High Forge in Session 38.

## Session History

- **[Session 1](/sessions/session-1)** — Dirk of the Stephen Guild offers the party 500 gold to guard a caravan along the Gold Road to Eldoran.
- **[Session 13](/sessions/session-13)** — Silas tracks the fleeing [Zeke](/npcs/zeke) from his burned cottage near Milstrom to the Gold Road, where Zeke had met riders with horses and gone beyond the party's reach toward Eldora.
- **[Session 38](/sessions/session-38)** — The party takes the road north out of High Forge through Redbrook, detours up the mountain to the Goliath village, then heads east toward Greyport.
