# Little Hovel

*Dive Metal Club • Market Ward, High Forge • Where Doomfall Broke Out*

## Overview

**The Little Hovel** is a dive metal club in the Market Ward of [High Forge](/locations/high-forge). [Helisanna](/player-characters/helisanna) played her breakout show there on the campaign's first night, winning [Doomfall](/organizations/doomfall) a 10,000-gold concert. The club is also tied to the [Twilight Company](/organizations/twilight-company): the party looked for the Company's [Victor](/npcs/victor) there during the dragon attack. Under Iron Claws rule it became an underground music venue for the [ravers](/organizations/ravers), where Helisanna rallied them in Session 32. Its state since the liberation of High Forge has not been described. Recaps and the table also call it "the Little Hobble."

## History

- **The first show (Session 1).** Helisanna scouted the club, signed up for a 1 AM slot after a run of terrible opening acts, and headlined with Bru's pyrotechnics and Red's purple-tinted Thaumaturgy. Audience members' eyes glowed purple, and talent agent Sandra Talks booked the band for a 10,000-gold show.
- **The Twilight Company (Sessions 3 and 7).** The Session 3 recap places an open Twilight Company meeting — Silas attending under the alias Martin Birchwood — beneath a bar it calls the Little Hobble. When dragons attacked the Market Ward in Session 7, the party drove to the Little Hovel looking for Victor and found him trying to save an armful of odd belongings, including a folder of old mining maps that got them out of the city.
- **The raver venue (Session 32).** With High Forge under the Iron Claws, the Little Hobble was an underground music venue full of purple-eyed ravers. Detective [Chalk Rock](/npcs/chalk-rock) was there investigating the disappearance of [April Bonal](/npcs/april-bonal), and Helisanna, despite warnings about her influence, played a set that commanded the crowd to spread the word and prepare for a massive concert in three days. The venue emptied as the ravers went to work.

## Session History

- **[Session 1](/sessions/session-1)** — Helisanna headlines the club's late set, her audience's eyes glow purple, and Silas lands a 10,000-gold concert with Sandra Talks.
- **[Session 3](/sessions/session-3)** — The party attends an open Twilight Company meeting held beneath the Little Hobble.
- **[Session 7](/sessions/session-7)** — During the dragon attack the party drives to the Little Hovel and rescues Victor, along with his folder of old tunnel maps.
- **[Session 32](/sessions/session-32)** — At the Little Hobble the party meets Chalk Rock, and Helisanna commands the ravers to prepare for the uprising concert.
