# Time Gun

*Artifact (schematics) • Schematics stolen by Helisanna; whereabouts unknown since Interlude 11*

## Overview

The **time gun** is a time-travel device from the wrecked world the party visited in Interlude 11. You travel through time by shooting yourself with it, and its invention destroyed that world. Helisanna stole its schematics from Marcus's tower. Where those schematics are now has not come up since. The party's worry is that [Scarlet](/npcs/scarlet), a dimensional scientist in this world, will build one. As of Session 63 they shut her down "every time she starts to invent a time gun".

## Properties

As Marcus described it in Interlude 11:

- The user must shoot themself with it to travel.
- It can only reach "nodes", critical decision points in time.
- It runs on bullets: going backward uses them, going forward restores them.
- Its schematics show how to use explosions to tear through space-time.

## The Time Gun Timeline

In Interlude 11 the old dragon Sol sent the party to a shattered world in the Astral Sea, held together by the artist Scarlet's paintings and the roots of the dying world tree Matthew Broadtwig. The chronomancer Marcus told them the time gun had wrecked their world, and that he, Scarlet and Matthew had spent two millennia trying every combination of choices to undo it. Every timeline ended with someone creating the time gun. Marcus had one bullet left and was preparing to shoot himself back to the start again.

The gun gets built in every timeline, but not always by the same hands: in one it was Marcus, in another an alternate Scarlet. One of them always builds it. In this world the roles are reversed: here Scarlet is the time-and-space scientist and Marcus the painter (Session 25), which is why the party keeps a close eye on her.

## The Party and Scarlet

- **Session 25:** the party recruited Scarlet on condition that she drop her temporal experiments and help build the [pocket village](/locations/pocket-village) instead.
- **Session 38:** Silas raised his worry about Scarlet's inventions, and the party agreed to keep her on existing projects.
- **Session 63:** Bru warned Olivia that Scarlet is "bad news" and never to let her near "the gun". Silas explained the other world's story; the party is "aggressively shutting her down every time she starts to invent a time gun".
