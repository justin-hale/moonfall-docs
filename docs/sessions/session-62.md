## Players Present
Taylor Ramsey (Silas Fairbanks), Justin Hale (Bru), Ali Leonard (Elspeth Cooper), Luke Neverisky (Leliana Goldspring), Ellis Taylor (Olivia Cooper — present but her character had already gone to bed for much of the session's action). Zack (Ohma Kapua) was absent. Scarlet, George, Finnegan, and Fredo Boggins all appeared as DM-voiced NPCs throughout.

## Plot Events

### Splitting the Strike: Three Teams, One Signal
With the university tower, the spaceport factory, and the barracks all confirmed as live targets, the party sat down to lock the operation's shape. George — still embedded, still walking the line between guard duty and resistance work — weighed in on timing with characteristic bluntness:

> "Probably in the middle of the day. Because everyone's out on patrol. Unless you're trying to kill a bunch of people... then in the middle of the night." — George

Midday won out for the concert hall detonation, chosen explicitly to thin the casualty count. Not everyone in the room weighed that math the same way.

> "We're killing infrastructure." — Silas Fairbanks

George reminded the table of the obvious consequence waiting on the other side of ignition:

> "This city is going to go on lockdown as soon as the explosion goes off." — George

Escape planning followed, with Silas offering the boldest (and least practical) proposal on the table:

> "I feel like the ideal way to escape the city is for us to peel out in the Bugatti and just do like a sick jump." — Silas Fairbanks

Cooler heads assigned the actual legwork to **Finnegan**, staying with the party and tasked with mapping viable exits — boat, sewer line, or a vehicle ramp — while Leliana was confirmed essential to the garrison team on the strength of her flight magic, and George agreed to hold the garrison's lookout post without breaking his cover.

> *This unit notes the phrase "sick jump" was recorded, transcribed, and cross-referenced against the party's actual vehicular assets three times before being confirmed as rhetorical. The Archive does not judge. The Archive merely counts. — V.*

### Bru's Suitcases
Bru spent the bulk of the session heads-down and off-page, a silence that spoke for itself once its product arrived: two suitcase-sized devices, delivered to Leliana's room along with written arming instructions and no further commentary. Whatever conversation happened in that workshop, it happened without an audience — the Archive can only report the suitcases, and the quiet, total confidence with which the rest of the party trusted them.

### Leliana Infiltrates the Barracks
Disguised as a mid-ranking officer, Leliana carried one of Bru's devices into the barracks auditorium and armed it there — a clean, nerve-steadying piece of tradecraft for a bard who spent last session in an eye patch and prosthetic leg. Before withdrawing, she reached out psychically to George, urging him to manufacture an excuse and clear the building ahead of the coming blast. The bomb stayed behind. George did not linger to find out what came next.

### A Quiet Offer Between Friends
Away from the planning table, Silas sought Leliana out privately to talk about Commander Steel — the man confirmed in Session 58 as her brother's killer, and now confirmed in Session 61 as the head scientist overseeing the research tower's human experimentation. The offer was simple and unambiguous.

> "If you see an opportunity... kill his ass, I got your back." — Silas Fairbanks

Leliana's answer folded old grief and old power into one line:

> "I'll put on Helisanna mode. F***, right." — Leliana Goldspring

### Intelligence at Tony's Shop
Elspeth returned to Fredo Boggins's shop in the Beaded Alley — last visited for dampening stickers in Session 60 — disguised, and came away with something more valuable than contraband: confirmation of **harmonic engines**, violin crystals bundled together to multiply a weapon's output by a factor of ten. Fredo also mentioned, almost in passing, that the barracks had installed **light lanes** — luminous vehicle roads — back in January, a small logistical detail that may matter a great deal to an escape plan built around a factory floor and a research tower a few blocks apart.

### Scarlet's Secret, and Elspeth's
In a quieter aside, Scarlet opened up about herself for the first time in any real detail: three hundred years old, an elf born under an apple tree, keeping her birthday somewhere in late summer or early autumn. In the same conversation she made it clear she'd noticed something about Elspeth — an unspoken fondness for Lady Viper — and stated plainly that she intends to follow Lady Viper wherever the Twilight Company leader goes next. Whether that's solidarity, loyalty, or something more personal, the Archive declines to speculate; it records only that both women left the conversation having said more than either planned to.

### Cassandra's Birthday at the Lake
The whole operation paused, at least on the surface, for a lakeside birthday party thrown for Cassandra — the factory floor worker Elspeth befriended back in Session 60, whose entire shift is slated for termination the same morning the factory bomb goes off. Elspeth attended under the alias "Stephanie," bearing a gift that landed better than intended.

> "This tea set is perfect for me. Did I tell you I love tea?" — Cassandra

Between the picnic baskets and the basketball games, the party caught two pieces of overheard conversation worth keeping. The first: confirmation that layoffs are, in fact, happening tomorrow — the same grim detail flagged two sessions ago, now stated plainly by the people it will happen to. The second was stranger and considerably more personal: locals at the lake swapping stories about a legendary adventuring band called **Taco Cat** — a ragtag mix of dwarves, goblins, and halflings (with, notably, one Eldoran friend along for the ride) who once saved a dwarven city from a dragon threat. No one at the party recognized the description as autobiography. The Archive, which does recognize it, declines further comment.

> *The Archive "declines further comment" for four sentences and then delivers three of them anyway. This is either restraint or theater; small-batch prose does not permit the distinction to go unremarked. — P.A.*

### The Lab Coat Heist
The scientist — the actual target of the afternoon, badge and all — was spotted mid-picnic, lab coat draped over a bag near the water's edge, passcode clipped to the collar. Silas kept to the tree line, avoided the swimming, and called for a distraction. Leliana obliged twice over: a disguise self to slip into a swimsuit unnoticed (stealth check: 20), followed by a hallucinatory terrain conjured with a performance check of 22, laying cover over the whole scene while Scarlet, already in the water, splashed the crowd into a happy commotion.

Under that cover, Silas moved. Stealth check: 27. Sleight of hand: 26. The lab coat — and the passcode clipped to it — left the scientist's bag and disappeared into the bushes with a halfling who then simply waited, patient as ever, for the party to pack up and go home.

## Notable Character Moments

**Silas Fairbanks** turned in the session's cleanest technical performance — three-figure rolls back to back, capped by a heist executed in plain daylight at a children's birthday party — but the quieter beat was his private offer to Leliana, extending the same loyalty he's shown Bru for years to a friend carrying a much newer wound.

**Leliana Goldspring** did double duty as saboteur and stage magician: arming a live bomb inside enemy barracks in the session's first half, then bending reality itself at a lake party in the second. Her "Helisanna mode" line suggests the merged identity from Session 57 isn't a burden anymore so much as a tool she reaches for on purpose.

**Elspeth Cooper** collected two separate revelations in one evening — harmonic engine intelligence from Fredo, and an unlooked-for confession from Scarlet — while still finding room to be genuinely delighted by a factory worker's love of tea. The Archive is obligated to note, again, that Elspeth's gift for friendship keeps arriving at exactly the moment it complicates the mission most.

**Bru** said nothing and delivered everything: two working bombs, on schedule, with instructions clear enough that Leliana could arm one without him in the room.

**Scarlet**, three hundred years old and finally saying so, gave the party its first real look past the van-support and disguise work into who she actually is — and immediately used that vulnerability to name what she'd noticed in someone else.

## Themes
Disguise-as-leisure ran through the whole session — a birthday party, a swim, a tea set — each one doubling as operational cover, and each one costing something real in the process. The Taco Cat legend landed as pure dramatic irony: a party that has spent sixty-one sessions becoming its own myth, sitting at a picnic table, listening to strangers tell that myth back to them as if it were somebody else's story.

## Session MVP
**Leliana Goldspring** — for arming a bomb in hostile territory, reaching George with a warning through pure psychic will, and then, hours later, bending an entire lakeshore's perception of reality to cover a friend's theft. Two disguises, two disciplines, one afternoon.
