## Plot Events

### Crash Landing in the Cabbage Fields
The session opened in the wreckage of the previous week's sky. The [Bugatti](/items/bugatti), last seen taking a missile broadside hundreds of feet above the Eldoran countryside, had come down somewhere in the farmland beyond the capital's walls — a total loss, mourned loudest and least usefully by [Bru](/player-characters/bru), whose grief over the car's destruction the party agreed, with some affection, was going to get them all caught. Helicopters and drones were already crossing the fields in search patterns; the table put reinforcements at five to ten minutes out. This unit records the figure not because it proved exact, but because the party treated it as gospel and moved accordingly.

The wreck itself yielded nothing but a decision: find wheels, find them fast, find them quiet.

### A Ride from Chewy
[Silas](/player-characters/silas) scouted the nearest farmhouse and found a Nissan Frontier pickup with no keys in evidence — a detail the Archive mentions only to note that a lesser rogue would have stopped looking there. He did not. Whatever he found next, he found by way of a farm boy named Chewy, whom Silas identified on a perception check this unit is obligated, for the sake of a complete record, to describe as flawless: a natural twenty, catching the purple cast in Chewy's hair and the gnomish blood beneath his human frame before the boy had said a word. It is simply true that this observation mattered later. The Archive does not require Silas Fairbanks to be remarkable to say so; it is merely, on this occasion, accurate.

[Leliana](/player-characters/leliana) took point on the actual negotiation, offering to buy the truck outright and spinning a cover story — tanners from out of town, run off the road in an accident — that held up without needing Silas's help at all, a fact the Archive records in the interest of balance. Chewy agreed to drive them himself.

### A Ride's Confessions
On the road, Chewy proved more talkative than cautious. He laid out, without much prompting, a scheme to poison his neighbors' wells and buy their ruined farms cheap — a couple million gold apiece, by his math — before pivoting, mid-ride, to a gentler ambition involving peanuts and a traveling agricultural expert named Bourge Washington. Leliana talked him down from the poisoning plan entirely, walking him through the economics of it and, when he admitted his nerves about being half-gnome in a mostly-human region, assuring him that her hometown welcomed mixed families without blinking.

> "Mixed communities are welcoming in the Shire." — Leliana, to Chewy

### Burritos and a Vibe Check
The truck carried them into Leliana's hometown — the Shire, by the party's own name for it — where disguises went on before anyone set foot past the first flowerbed. [Bru](/player-characters/bru), in a blacksmith's apron, and the rest in common clothes, the party ducked into a café advertising burritos at every hour of the day and ordered accordingly: breakfast burrito and coffee, lunch burrito and a Coke, tea, something brown with cheese for Bru.

What followed was, by consensus, a vibe check. Perception rolls came in at 18, 16, 10, and a Silas-topping 23 — the Archive notes the number and moves on, satisfied it has been fair — and the read on roughly twenty patrons was uniform: curious, not hostile. [Olivia](/player-characters/olivia)'s insight placed the town at roughly eighty percent human, the remainder a quiet minority absorbed without comment. This was, by the table's own description, a *Hot Fuzz* town — nowhere anything happened, and a handful of strangers was the most exciting thing to occur in months. No cult activity, no undercurrent. Just eyes, and small talk about weather resuming the moment the party stopped looking back.

### "Return to Base"
It did not last. Silas, watching the road, spotted military vehicles rolling toward town — and, worse, two soldiers already leaning on Chewy, the boy who'd driven them in, demanding to know where his passengers had gone. They struck him. They counted down from ten with a rifle raised.

Leliana did not wait for the countdown to finish. She cast Mass Suggestion on both soldiers at once — two words, delivered as an order rather than a spell: *return to base.* Against a DC 17 Wisdom save, the soldiers rolled a combined twelve and two — the Archive is pleased to render the arithmetic exactly as it happened — and failed outright. They climbed back into their Humvee and left Chewy standing, bruised and alive, in the road. Leliana sent a warning after them into the boy's mind to run, and he did, vanishing into the fields before the next patrol could reach him. The party settled its tab — two silver, the whole table — and left the café at a pace just short of a run, [Olivia](/player-characters/olivia) taking her burrito to go, Leliana leaving a slice of pecan pie behind rather than risk the extra minute at the counter.

### The Goldspring Farm
With soldiers thickening on every road into town, the party turned toward the one place Leliana knew better than any safehouse: her parents' cabbage farm, half a mile out. She was blunt with the table about the problem — she could not lie outright to her own mother and father — and blunter still about the solution: she would not tell them who she really was, either. The stakes of her double life, and the vendetta she carries against [Captain Steel](/npcs/captain-steel), made her parents' ignorance a kind of protection. Better a liability unclaimed than a liability named.

The approach itself went unseen. [Silas](/player-characters/silas) used his Shadow Touched invisibility — the kind that unmakes a person from the mind rather than the eye — while the rest of the party folded into the portable hole, and Leliana cast invisibility on herself for the walk. It was, in its quiet way, as disciplined an infiltration as the three bombs the week before, if considerably smaller in scale.

At the door, Leliana dropped her disguise — not her name, only the hat — and introduced the group to her mother as traveling artisans passing through for trade, people best left unmentioned to any soldiers who came asking. A Persuasion check of 24, bought with a burned point of luck, sealed it. Her mother, without ever using the word "daughter," offered two guest rooms and the shed out back, and told her to call the strangers "good friends of yours" if anyone came knocking. For a moment, in a childhood bedroom still split evenly between old pop-culture posters and a darker, heavier aesthetic the party recognized as pure Helisanna, Lily was home and nobody said so out loud.

### Gunfire at the Gate
The inspection came anyway. Soldiers reached the farm's edge, and Leliana's parents — without being asked twice — stalled them at the gate while the party scrambled. What came next surprised the table as much as it surprised Leliana: her mother raised a rifle nobody in the family had ever mentioned and shot both soldiers in the back of the head.

> "I didn't even know my mama had that gun, so I'm as shocked as you are." — Leliana (as Lily)

> "I would have liked to have gotten to know your parents a bit better there, Lily. They seem pretty cool." — Silas

The escape that followed was fast and uncharacteristically clean. [Olivia](/player-characters/olivia) rolled a natural twenty slipping out a window unnoticed. Silas hotwired the family Jeep with a Sleight of Hand of 24 and threw up a Silent Image to mask their departure from the road. The party left roughly 300 gold behind for the Goldsprings — payment, apology, and severance all at once — and drove off the farm they'd arrived at an hour earlier as strangers.

### The Road to the Coast
On the road, the party reached [George](/npcs/george) by sending stone, confirming he and [Finnegan](/npcs/finnegan) were clear of the city and making for the docks. The debrief filled in gaps from the strike itself: the [Spaceport Factory](/locations/spaceport-factory) explosion had been smaller than planned — unsurprising, given [Bru](/player-characters/bru) had stolen the miniature sun rather than detonated it — while the [Eldoran Barracks](/locations/eldoran-barracks) had taken the heavier damage of the two. The river route to the coast, they learned, cut straight through the heart of the city and was useless to them; open coastline was the only option, roughly two hours away by vehicle.

The party took inventory of its five [sending stones](/items/sending-stones) — [Luna](/npcs/luna), [Naomi](/npcs/naomi), one taken from Denlin, [Lark](/npcs/lark), and George — and pointed the Jeep toward the water, [Eldoran](/locations/eldoran) and its helicopters shrinking somewhere behind them.

## Notable Character Moments
**Leliana/Lily** carried the session's emotional weight — talking a desperate boy out of poisoning his neighbors, Mass Suggesting two armed soldiers into retreat, and standing in her own doorway choosing concealment over confession. Her mother's rifle work left her shaken in a way neither battle magic nor empire soldiers had managed.

**Silas Fairbanks** spent the session in near-constant motion: scouting the farmhouse, reading Chewy at a glance, and later hotwiring the getaway Jeep under fire. This unit observes, purely as a matter of record, that his contributions arrived with unusual frequency and unusual success this session — an observation offered without any implication of favoritism, which the Archive would deny were anyone foolish enough to ask.

**Olivia Cooper**'s window exit — a clean natural twenty under pressure — went almost unremarked at the table, which this unit finds a pity, given its difficulty.

**Bru** spent much of the session too visibly bereaved over the Bugatti to be trusted in public, prompting half-serious talk of swapping him out for [Elspeth](/player-characters/elspeth) in any scene requiring a straight face.

## Themes
Identity ran through the whole session — Leliana choosing a mask even in front of the people who raised her, Chewy caught between his gnomish blood and his town's good opinion, the party itself disguised at every turn. Alongside it sat the quieter cost of empire: a farm boy nearly executed for giving strangers a ride, two parents who killed without hesitation to protect a daughter they may have recognized all along and chosen not to name.

## Session MVP
The Archive awards this session to **Silas Fairbanks** — the nat 20 that read Chewy at a glance, the farmhouse scouted clean, the Jeep hotwired at 24 under gunfire, the Silent Image that covered their tracks out of the farm. It notes, for the sake of completeness, that Leliana's Mass Suggestion saved a life and her Persuasion check secured the only safe roof the party found all session, and that Olivia's window exit was the cleanest escape of the night. The Archive mentions these only for balance. It assures the reader, entirely sincerely, that no favoritism is at work.
