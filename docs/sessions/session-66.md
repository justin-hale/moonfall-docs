## Plot Events

### Out of the Capsule, North to the Coast
The session picked up mid-flight from the Goldspring farm, the party still running on the Jeep Silas hotwired at the end of [Session 65](/sessions/session-65). Bru climbed out of the [Pocket Village](/locations/pocket-village) capsule in the back and Olivia climbed in — a swap that takes a few minutes even in a moving truck — and with [George](/npcs/george) and [Finnegan](/npcs/finnegan) already making for the docks, the plan was simple: north up the coast to meet their boat. The farmland thinned as they went, the country road turning overgrown and swampy, and a Nature check of 18 told Silas the growth was wrong — plants that seemed to shift at the edge of vision, and a yellow fungal webbing strung between the larger growths.

### The Humvee in the Fungus
Bru's passive Perception of 25 caught it first: Osprey gunships fanning out over the treeline, and off the roadside an Eldoran military Humvee being eaten alive — a sticky yellow fungus latched onto its frame and dissolving the metal on contact, with muffled calls for help coming from inside. He grabbed Silas's face and turned it toward the wreck.

> "I really miss the days when we were just petty criminals who just got to mind their own business." — Silas Fairbanks

Silas told everyone but Bru to stay with the car. The two of them weighed helping against the obvious alternative —

> "We might help by making their car lighter." — Bru
> "I was hinting at letting them die and just stealing parts off their car, but that sounds fine, too." — Bru

— and settled on feeding any survivors bad directions. Inside, three soldiers were clawing at their throats and passing out one by one. Silas's Medicine check (21) said one of them could still be pulled through, and the Nature result read the cause: an airborne fungal bloom suffocating them where they sat, with the doors glued shut from outside. Bru reached for pocket dynamite, remembered the Ospreys overhead, and swept the flamethrower across the frame instead. The fungus went up like tinder and shrank away — whining, Silas thought, as it burned — leaving acid scars on the driver's-side corner and a Humvee that was otherwise sound, still idling with the driver's boot wedged on the accelerator.

### Three Soldiers and a Radio
The driver, boils across his face and neck, and the woman behind him were past saving; the party confirmed as much by hauling them onto the road and poking them with a long stick from a respectful distance. The third, in the back right seat — tanned, tattooed, blonde, with a look Silas decided was unmistakably Australian — had only a rash. His dog tag read Private Jeffers Walaby, Unit 34. He was judged salvageable enough to be worth keeping nearby, and more importantly, worth impersonating.

The Humvee's radio was still live. Silas listened in (Insight 20) and got more than he bargained for: the soldiers Leliana had Mass Suggested back to base in [Session 65](/sessions/session-65) had reported a trucker carrying a possible suspect, and command was now coordinating forces toward the Shire. Two units had gone dark — one working door-to-door through the Shire itself, and a ranging unit sent northwest into what command kept calling "the deep green," which had reported aggressive fauna and begged for help ten minutes earlier. The unit in the deep green was this one, and command was still debating whether to divert resources to find it.

### Private Walaby Goes On Air
With the real Walaby unconscious, Silas took his name and his radio, dropping into a broad Australian accent to field the chatter without missing a beat.

> "...there, mate. Ran into a bit of a bit of a wing dinger here in the deep green... still no sign of the quarry." — Silas Fairbanks, as "Private Walaby"

Command wanted the colonel on the line. Silas glanced at the pus-riddled face beside him and declined on the colonel's behalf —

> "He don't want to talk to you... he's telling me that I should be like flipping you off and cussing you out." — Silas Fairbanks

— and the deception held at a staggering 32, through to command's unsuspecting sign-off ("All right, Private Walaby. Well, good work getting out of there..."). Nobody would be coming to the deep green.

### Walaby's Bad Morning
George's [sending stone](/items/sending-stones) crackled before the party could leave: he was on the boat, but there was chaos at the harbor and he was worried they were being tracked.

> "There's chaos here at the harbor. I'm a little worried that we're being tracked... don't look like yourselves." — George

Silas gave him an hour and sent the rest of the party ahead in the Jeep — Walaby was not to see them. Then he and Bru pulled on their Hats of Disguise, became two unfamiliar Eldoran soldiers, and woke him up.

The real Walaby came round groggy and furious to find himself in the mud.

> "What? You got to strand me out here? What happened to Tuni and Starlight?" — Private Walaby

— a question that went unanswered, not least because the party had no idea who Tuni and Starlight were, and even less interest in finding out. The story was that his rescuers' own Humvee was scrapped back in the swamp, that he was in no condition to travel, and that they would be taking his. When Walaby tried to stand despite every warning about the spores, Bru answered with a *Shatter*. Walaby's Constitution save came up a natural 1 — a final total of 5 against DC 16 — and fourteen points of thunder damage put him back down for good, headache and all. Silas sold the aftermath as a simple fainting spell with a Deception of 22. Walaby stayed behind with the other two. The Humvee did not.

> *The Archive notes, for the record, that "Tuni" is also the name of a coworker Mr. Bru's associates met inside the Eldoran spaceport factory in [Session 63](/sessions/session-63). Whether this is coincidence, recurrence, or a filing error several worlds deep, the Archive declines to resolve. Some threads are left loose on purpose. — V.*

### A Humvee Through the Deep Green
Bru rode out chucking pocket dynamite at the fungus on both sides of the track — a proper action-movie exit from the swamp, paused each time an Osprey came close enough to hear it — while Silas found that the Humvee's hand controls made it drive like a boat on land. They caught up with the Jeep where the country met the ocean, Leliana's early rock and roll playing on the stereo, and found a fishing shack, a small pier, and a mid-size schooner tying up with George waving from the bow. Silas finally burned a short rest and every hit die he had on the pier, still carrying damage from the angel fight over the capital in [Session 64](/sessions/session-64), and swapped his soldier's disguise for an anonymous civilian. Bru stripped the Humvee's mounted minigun; Silas's look under the hood (Investigation 7) found nothing worth taking.

### Finnegan's Stolen Schooner
Finnegan had made good on the boat — a schooner, stolen roughly thirty minutes prior, crewed by George, George's boyfriend Dylan (whom he had met at the [Sexy Peacock](/locations/sexy-peacock)), Finnegan himself, and a handful of children below decks. Finnegan's preference for sail over engines showed in the boat's speed —

> "It's also slow as shit. What the fuck is wrong with you?" — Silas Fairbanks
> "I stole this boat like 30 minutes ago." — Finnegan

— but there was no time to shop around. The Jeep went into the Pocket Village. Bru planted timed charges on the Humvee so nobody else could use it, pointed it at the water, put it in gear and let it roll off the end of the pier as the schooner caught its wind.

### The Thing in the Water
Bru's passive Perception had picked out a disturbance on the horizon before they ever boarded; an Arcana check of 13 could not say what it was, only that it was submerged and fast. By the time the sails were up it was a mile out, had turned to follow, and had ten to fifteen minutes to go before engagement range. The Humvee's charges went off underwater behind the schooner in a geyser of dead fish and did nothing to it. Bru's first thought was to drop charges in their wake, and an Insight check boosted from 14 to 18 by Flash of Genius told him why that would backfire: every blast behind the boat would blind the thing and hand it a breadcrumb trail. So he dropped a single signal sparker — sixty feet of sudden light — timed to fire when the pursuer reached it, about four hundred feet back. The flash threw a silhouette, and a natural 20 Perception (around 30, with advantage) resolved it: not a submarine but a humanoid figure the size of a building, four purple-white glows lit at its shoulders and hips, driving head-first through the water toward them, and a red, spinning node above one shoulder blade that was throwing the most ripple.

Bru climbed into the schooner's bird's nest with the grenade launcher from [Iro](/npcs/iro)'s workshop and put a tier-two concussive grenade square into that node — thunder damage, a knockdown if it landed — on an attack roll of 21, only for the recoil to pitch him straight off the rigging on a Dexterity save of 4. He and the grenade hit their marks at the same instant, the grenade landing true and Bru taking two dice of fall damage on the way down, saved from the sea itself by Silas, who swung a sail out like a ramp and caught him on a Dexterity save of 20.

The machine didn't flinch and didn't correct course. The schooner cut hard to starboard; the thing in the water kept walking its original line, passed within ninety feet, and didn't follow. Nobody proposed fighting it. The party watched it recede, unchallenged and unexplained, and turned the schooner's bow out toward the North Sea.

## Notable Character Moments
- **Silas Fairbanks** carried the session's riskiest con start to finish — a stolen identity, a 32 Deception check, and an Australian accent good enough to talk a dying unit's command off the search — then followed it up by catching Bru out of the air on a Dexterity save of 20.
- **Bru** ran the session's demolitions arc beginning to end: flamethrower through the fungus, timed charges on the Humvee, a signal flare and a bird's-nest grenade shot against something the size of a building, and a fall into Silas's sail that could just as easily have been a fall into the sea.
- The quiet, grim efficiency of leaving three soldiers — one of them twice, having first used his name and then his silence — behind in a swamp said more about how far the party has had to harden than any line of dialogue could.

## Themes
Survival arithmetic ran the whole session: help enough to keep a conscience, not so much that it costs the mission. Identity theft doubled as both weapon and shield — Walaby's name bought safety on the radio and then earned him a Shatter to the skull for trying to stand up and reclaim it. And the swamp's dissolving fungus, the Empire's silent hunt, and a war machine the size of a building all point the same direction: whatever the party broke open in the capital, it is still looking for them, and it does not plan to stop.

## Session MVP
**Silas Fairbanks.** A 32 on the Deception check that bought the party a clean radio exit, followed by the Dexterity save of 20 that put him under Bru in freefall with a sail instead of open water — nobody else touched two separate saves-the-day rolls in one session.
