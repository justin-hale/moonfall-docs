# Eldoran Empire

*Theocratic Empire • Worshippers of Eldora • Violescence Technology*

## Overview

The **Eldoran Empire**, also called the **Golden Empire**, is a militarised, technologically advanced empire built around the worship of the ascended goddess **Eldora**. Its knights and paladins wear armour and weapons set with **violescence** crystals. It forces conquered people to give up their old gods, and it has a space station whose weapon shot down the moon goddess [Luna](/npcs/luna). It rules from its capital, [Eldoran](/locations/eldoran). [Greyport](/locations/greyport), capital of the Free Republic, is one of the last places still holding out against it.

**Status as of Session 63:** [Taco Cat](/organizations/taco-cat), working for the [Twilight Company](/organizations/twilight-company), has just hit three targets in the capital at once. The university [research tower](/locations/research-tower) collapsed across the city wall, charges went off at the [barracks](/locations/eldoran-barracks) concert hall and the [spaceport factory](/locations/spaceport-factory), and [Bru](/player-characters/bru) stole the factory's power core, a miniature sun. The party is fleeing the city. The space station and its weapon are untouched.

## Eldora and the Two Moons

- Eldora is a goddess who **ascended**, and the Empire's second moon is an artificial satellite created when she did (Sessions 13–14). Luna later described that moon as a temple, a generator and a weapon (Session 46).
- Imperial soldiers demand that people swear allegiance to "Empress Eldora" and abandon their gods (Session 8). The knight Eric spoke of extinguishing all other gods (Session 16). Bafael later summarised the goal: kill the gods so that Eldora grows stronger (Session 41).
- In Session 16 the party saw the real moon crack apart and a vast female figure fall from the sky. This was the moon goddess the party now knows as Luna, and they took it as Eldora wiping out a rival god (Session 17). Luna herself describes a massive white beam fired from a giant dish on the side of the space station, which the party named the **"space bowl"** (Session 46). It shattered her "in the weave", beyond her physical form.
- A demon, Velz'eth, told the party that the fae [Crowley](/npcs/crowley) was working with the "Moon Queen", Eldora, toward a world of "cold steel and blind salvation" (Interlude X).

## Military

- **Knights and paladins:** Glowing, crystal-armoured knights patrol the roads between cities (Session 24). They staff checkpoints like the one that warned travellers away from [Milstrom](/locations/milstrom) (Session 10). The Session 16 knight represented the **Order of Eldora**, and the Razlemania faction called themselves the **Knights of Eldora** (Session 51).
- **Crystal shields:** Golden-armoured soldiers linked crystal shields into a dome that protected thousands of civilians during the dragon attack on [High Forge](/locations/high-forge) (Session 7). The DM later confirmed these were Eldoran arcane shields (Session 38 transcript).
- **Violescence gear:** The knights at Luna's remains each wore six crystals and their captain wore fifteen. The crystals glow when their power is used (Session 52). At Razlemania the Eldoran wrestlers used theirs telekinetically to cheat (Session 51).
- **Ascension:** In the barracks concert hall, entranced musicians and shackled foreign prisoners were used in a harmonic ritual. An ordinary soldier grew white-gold wings and a blazing greatsword in front of the crowd (Session 61).
- **Magic detection:** Soldiers wear laser-scanning helmets that flag magic items, components and unregistered spellcasters (Session 58). White-armoured patrols with antennae tally magical purchases shop by shop (Session 60).
- **Clones:** Six sleeping copies of [Elspeth](/player-characters/elspeth) were found in flight simulators in the research tower, being trained as shock troopers for the Empire's war with the dragons (Session 63).
- **Space:** Radio broadcasts came from a space station near the moon (Session 14) and advertised recruitment for an "Eldoran Space Force" (Session 16). [Victor](/npcs/victor) has tracked the station's resupply teleport sites (Session 45).

## Harmonic Resonance and Violescence

The research tower's acoustics floor explained how the Empire works (Session 61). Its researchers use sound and music as a model of how the physical, astral and divine planes connect. **Violescence** makes that **harmonic resonance** possible, drawing power from belief into the material world. Belief feeds the god and the god feeds the believer, a loop that grows without limit if left unchecked. Their notes cite studies of [Y'chek](/npcs/ychek), "a god of passion and music" and Helisanna's patron, as the original template, and flag dragon orbs as a possible way to disrupt the system. The top floor held experiments on living subjects.

- The moon weapon grew out of research by Lieutenant **Steven Goldspring**, [Leliana](/player-characters/leliana)'s brother. He refused to hand over his "harmonic alignment" work, and **[Captain Steel](/npcs/captain-steel)** killed him for it. The weapon turned his research from containing uncontainable energy to aiming it (Session 57, a memory Leliana relived).
- **Harmonic engines** bundle crystals together to multiply a weapon's output tenfold, according to [Fredo Boggins](/npcs/fredo-boggins) (Session 62).
- The party has come to believe the Empire is stockpiling violescence toward something catastrophic, and that another universe was already destroyed this way (Session 48).

## The Capital

[Eldoran](/locations/eldoran) is carved into two facing cliffsides and built out across the top, and it is reached by elevator from the valley floor (Session 55). Inside are hovercraft, drones, a harbour spaceport launching shuttles, and citizens with red-glowing cybernetic eyes. Five giant floating crystals arranged in a pentagon, one of them at the spaceport, beam power down to pylons across the city. The inner ring is affluent and mostly human. In the outer rings dwarves run shops, halflings, gnomes and orcs work as labour, and goblins are absent from every work crew (Session 57). In the capital the god Tony is followed only as a philosophy, there being "no god but Eldora" (Session 60).

Neighbouring **Bradicus** is an independent state whose status the Empire contests. The Bradicus family made the guns "that built the Golden Empire" (Session 55).

## Agents, Allies and Enemies

- **[Captain Steel](/npcs/captain-steel):** Gaunt, half-machine officer who murdered Steven Goldspring. He is stationed at the university research centre (Session 58) and was seen at the spaceport factory (Sessions 60 and 63).
- **Eric:** A female Eldoran knight whose crystals had gone dull and black. She helped the party against a Shadowfell incursion (Session 16), then led the attack on the Skyhammer village and was killed by [Helisanna](/player-characters/helisanna)'s Eldritch Blast (Session 22). A "Captain Sir Eric" later led Lady Corwin's mercenaries through High Forge (Session 37). The recaps do not say whether this is the same person.
- **[Zeke](/npcs/zeke):** An Eldoran spy who poisoned the well of [Red](/player-characters/red)'s adoptive parents and fled toward Eldoran (Session 13).
- **[Order of St. George](/organizations/order-of-st-george):** Dragon hunters working with the Empire from its moon base to turn the moon weapon on Noraxis, the dragon god and Luna's partner (Sessions 40–41).
- **[Lady Corwin](/npcs/lady-corwin) and the Blackstone family:** High Forge nobles with Eldoran ties. Corwin planned to hand High Forge and its violescence to the Empire (Session 31). Her mercenary army arrived a month after her death, looked the city over and reported it "sacked and nothing worth saving" (Session 37).
- **[Skyhammer Clan](/organizations/skyhammer-clan):** According to the clan, Eldoran knights attacked their village when Red was an infant and took their scientists and technology (Session 17). Eldoran soldiers were later found dismantling the abandoned village with a teleport "threshold" device (Session 38).
- **[Jasper Gaines](/player-characters/jasper):** A former Eldoran soldier who turned on Eric to defend the Goliaths (Session 22). He now runs a brewery in Greyport and says the Empire "lost their way" once it started killing gods (Session 48).
- **Opponents:** The [Twilight Company](/organizations/twilight-company), whose founder [Lady Viper](/npcs/lady-viper) lost her family to the Empire about fifty years ago (Session 47), and [Taco Cat](/organizations/taco-cat).

## Conflict with Taco Cat

- **Luna:** The party freed Luna's remains from an Eldoran chain-and-portal rig, killed its captain, and brought her back to life (Session 52). The next night [Leliana](/player-characters/leliana)'s concert raised a new moon over Greyport, and the crowd began talking about resistance (Session 53).
- **The infiltration:** [Lady Viper](/npcs/lady-viper) briefed the party on the capital's magic detection (Session 54). They crossed an Eldoran checkpoint as the mercenary company Pupusa Possum (Session 54), competed in [Lord Bradicus](/npcs/lord-bradicus)'s contest (Sessions 55–56), and surfaced in the capital through a World Tree (Session 57).
- **The strike:** [George](/npcs/george), the Twilight Company's plant in the city guard, named three targets: the spaceport factory, the university research centre and the barracks (Session 58). The party scouted all three (Sessions 59–61), planted the charges (Sessions 62–63) and set them off together (Session 63).
